## Overview

Made-to-order furniture quickly runs into SKU explosion when modeled naively. Sera Muebles unifies standalone pieces and sets under a slot-based customization model mapped directly to workshop finish libraries.

## Architecture & Features

- **Interactive 3D & WebAR Customizer**: Real-time React Three Fiber viewport with instant PBR texture swapping across woods, fabrics, and metals—plus QR desktop handoff, Apple Quick Look (`.usdz`), and Google Scene Viewer (`.glb`).
- **In-Browser glTF/GLB Optimizer**: Custom binary parser (`glbOptimizer.ts`) that downscales heavy textures and sanitizes discontinuous UV attributes or extensions unsupported by mobile AR viewers.
- **Atomic Checkout & Webhooks**: Server-authoritative order creation in Deno Edge Functions (`create-checkout`, `payment-webhook`), HMAC signature verification for Mercado Pago, and idempotent PostgreSQL state transitions.
- **Atelier Admin (`/admin`)**: Auth-gated backoffice featuring Recharts analytics, a multi-tab product editor, bulk 3D ZIP package import, and order/inquiry management.
